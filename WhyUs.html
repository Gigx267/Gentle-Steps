What Sets Us Apart

Built Different. On Purpose.

Verified, Not Just Listed

Every clinician passes a five-stage verification: credential audit, pathway evidence, clinical interview, telehealth readiness and ongoing supervision. No self-reported qualifications.

Specialists, Not Generalists

Grief work, trauma therapy and corporate burnout are different skill sets. We match you to a clinician with documented training in your specific pathway — not whoever has an opening.

Transparent Naira Pricing

Every rate is published. No "contact us for a quote." No hidden fees. Naira-locked for 90 days, with a sliding scale for lower-income households and a 10% reserve.

Low-Data, Real-World Design

Chat sessions use 5–10 MB. Audio sessions use 15–20 MB. Our clinicians are trained to handle dropped connections, load-shedding and shared rooms — because that's real life.

Anti-Shame Approach

We never use fear as a sales tool. Relapse, self-harm and family conflict are discussed without judgment. Therapy is offered, not guaranteed — and we say so.

Privacy as Standard

Health data is treated as sensitive personal data under NDPA 2023. Explicit consent required. Granular permissions. Session notes are never shared with whoever paid for the session.

The Standard We Hold

What We Refuse to Compromise On

No Unverified Practitioners

We reject 86% of clinician applicants. That's not a marketing number — it's a clinical safeguard. If someone cannot demonstrate supervised training in their claimed pathway, they don't see our clients. Full stop.

No Hidden Fees, Ever

The price you see is the price you pay. Gateway fees are absorbed by us. There are no "platform fees," "booking charges" or "cancellation penalties" beyond the stated 24-hour policy.

No Pretending We're Emergency Care

We are not an emergency service and we never pretend to be. If your safety screening flags imminent risk, we pause the booking and connect you to crisis resources. That's the responsible thing to do.

No Fear-Based Marketing

We never use distress as a sales lever. No countdown timers on therapy packages, no "you'll regret not booking" messaging. You come to us when you're ready, not when we pressure you.
