Quick Answers

Before You Book

How much does a session cost?

Entry tier starts at ₦8,000 per 50-minute session for audio or chat-based work. Standard individual therapy is ₦15,000. Senior specialist trauma work is ₦25,000 and couples or family sessions are ₦30,000. Sliding-scale placements start at ₦5,000 flat. All rates are published on our pricing page and locked for 90 days.

Do you offer family therapy and corporate services?

Yes. Family therapy brings 3–6 members together over encrypted video — whether they're spread across Lagos, London and Houston, or living in the same compound — to address dynamics, parenting and shared trauma. Corporate and workplace mental health coaching is delivered virtually to teams, addressing employee burnout, workplace stress and mental wellness, with NGO and small-business discounts available. Both run on the same five-stage verified clinician network.

Can I do therapy without video, using only chat?

Yes, for Tier 1 and Tier 2 work. Text-based therapy uses only 5–10 MB per session and is often the only format available to someone in a shared household with no private space. There are two honest limitations: for complex trauma or high-risk situations, a clinician may need occasional voice contact to assess safety properly, and chat is not suitable for acute crisis.

What if I don't connect with my therapist?

Request a rematch from your dashboard. There is no charge, no penalty period, no waiting list, and no requirement to explain yourself to your current therapist. We brief the new clinician on your history so you don't have to retell the hardest parts from scratch. Around 31% of our clients switch at least once — and those who do typically report better outcomes afterwards.
