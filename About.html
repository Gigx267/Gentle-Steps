Our Story

It Started With a Waiting List Nobody Could Afford

Gentle Steps began with a simple, uncomfortable observation: the people who most needed specialised mental health care in Nigeria were the least likely to find it. Grief support existed, but rarely in Igbo. Trauma therapy existed, but rarely at a price a teacher could pay. Corporate wellness existed, but mostly as a poster in a break room.

Meanwhile, the platforms that did offer online therapy were built for a different reality entirely — credit cards, unlimited data, and a therapy culture that assumed you had your own quiet room. None of that described most of the people we knew.

So we built differently. We started with specialisation over generalisation, because grief work and workplace burnout are not the same skill. We added chat and audio sessions for people with no private space and a 2GB data plan. We made pricing public, naira-locked and sliding-scale. And we insisted that every clinician we onboard can actually demonstrate training in the work they claim to do.

Today we serve clients across Nigeria and the diaspora — but the founding principle has not moved: care before scale.

Mission, Vision & the Rules We Won't Break

Our Clinical Model

How Care Actually Runs Here

Not a marketplace where anyone can hang a shingle. A supervised clinical network with verification, pathways and outcome review built in.

The 5-Stage Verification Pipeline

Every clinician passes all five before a single client is matched to them.

1

Credential Audit

Degree certificates, licensure and professional body membership verified against issuing institutions.

2

Pathway Evidence

Documented supervised hours and training specific to the pathway they will practice — not general experience.

3

Clinical Interview

A structured case-based interview with our clinical lead, including risk assessment and escalation reasoning.

4

Telehealth Readiness

Assessment of remote-specific competence: managing silence on audio, reading distress over chat, low-bandwidth contingency.

5

Ongoing Supervision

Monthly group supervision and quarterly outcome review. Clinicians who stop meeting the standard are removed.

Trauma-Informed by Default

Not just trauma therapy — trauma-informed practice across every pathway. That means pacing, choice, predictability and never forcing disclosure. A client who is not ready to name what happened is not failing; the clinician adjusts.

Measured, Not Assumed

Each pathway carries its own outcome measures, reviewed at sessions 1, 3 and 6. If a client is not improving, we adjust the plan, the approach or the clinician — rather than letting someone quietly drift for months.

Built for Real Constraints

Load-shedding, shared rooms, expensive data, unreliable networks. Our clinicians are trained to run a meaningful session when the video drops, when the client is typing under a blanket, or when the power goes out mid-sentence.

Editorial Standards

How We Write About Mental Health

Everything published on this platform is written to be useful to someone in distress — not to rank, not to shock, and not to sell.

Clinical Review

Any page describing symptoms, treatment approaches or crisis guidance is reviewed by a licensed clinician before publication and re-reviewed at least annually. Reviewer names and credentials are recorded internally, and the review date is tracked against each page.

Sourcing

We prioritise peer-reviewed research, WHO and Nigerian federal health guidance, and professional body positions from bodies such as the Nigerian Association of Clinical Psychologists. Where evidence is mixed or contested, we say so rather than presenting a single view as settled fact.

Limits We State Clearly

We do not claim online therapy is right for everyone. Acute psychiatric crisis, active psychosis, and situations requiring physical safety intervention are outside what telehealth can responsibly provide. Where that is the case, we say so on the page rather than burying it in a disclaimer.

Corrections

If you believe something on this site is inaccurate, unsafe or misleading, email hello@gentlesteps.ng with the page and the concern. Clinical content corrections are reviewed within five working days and substantive changes are noted on the page itself.

Voice Guidelines

  • Plain language over clinical jargon — if a word needs a glossary, rewrite the sentence.
  • No fear-based marketing. Distress is never used as a sales lever.
  • Nigerian English spelling and naira-first pricing throughout.
  • Never promise outcomes. Therapy is offered, not guaranteed.

Data & Privacy Position

Health information is treated as sensitive personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, requiring explicit consent. African data residency is used where available. Consent is granular — you can accept care without accepting marketing, and you can request deletion at any time.

Safety & Governance

The Boring Parts That Protect You

Governance is not glamorous, but it is the difference between a therapy platform and a chat app.

Risk Escalation Protocol

Every booking includes a safety screen before payment. Where risk indicators appear, the booking is flagged for the clinical lead and the therapist contacts the client with a safety plan before the first session. Emergency contacts are only used where there is judged imminent risk, and the client is told first.

Supervision & Reflective Practice

Clinicians participate in monthly group supervision, with additional individual supervision for complex trauma and recovery cases. Supervision is a professional requirement here, not an optional extra — and it is how we catch drift before it affects a client.

Scope of Practice

No clinician on this platform practises outside their verified scope. If a client presents with needs better served by psychiatry, inpatient care or a specialist we do not have, we refer out and say so plainly — even though it means losing the booking.

Records & Confidentiality

Session notes are kept to the minimum required for continuity of care and stored with access limited to the treating clinician and the clinical lead. Notes are never shared with whoever paid for the session. Where a client is sponsored from abroad, the payer receives booking confirmation only.

Complaints & Rematch

A client who does not feel safe or well-matched can request a rematch from their dashboard without charge, without a penalty period and without having to justify it to the current clinician. Clinical complaints are reviewed by the clinical lead, not by the marketing team.

NDPA 2023 Alignment

We operate as a data controller processing sensitive personal data on the basis of explicit consent. Clients can access, correct, export or delete their data, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent for health data processing necessarily ends the care relationship, and we tell you that upfront rather than burying it.
