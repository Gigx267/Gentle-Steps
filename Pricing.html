Cancellation & Rescheduling

Clear Rules, Fairly Applied

Life happens. Our cancellation policy is designed to protect your therapist's time without punishing you for an emergency.

24+ hours' notice

Full refund to your account, or free rescheduling to any available slot. No questions asked, no penalties, no limits on how often.

Under 24 hours

Half the session fee is retained to compensate the clinician's reserved time. The other half is credited to your account for a future session.

Genuine emergencies

Bereavement, illness, accident, security incident — tell us and the standard policy is waived. We would rather trust clients than police them.

Running late or having connection trouble?

Message your therapist in the session room if you can, or contact support. If your session has to start late because of your connection, your therapist will make every effort to run the full time. If the network drops entirely, the session is rescheduled without charge.

Pricing Questions

Before You Pay

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The only additional charge disclosed anywhere on the platform is a transparent 3.9% surcharge on international cards, which is shown at checkout before you confirm. Local payment gateway fees are absorbed by Gentle Steps. There are no booking fees, platform surcharges, or add-ons revealed later.

How does the sliding scale work?

The Open Door programme reserves 10% of every month's sessions for sliding-scale placements, allocated by need rather than speed of application. Households earning below ₦100,000 per month pay ₦5,000 flat. Households earning ₦100,000–₦200,000 pay ₦8,000 flat. Households earning ₦200,000–₦300,000 pay ₦10,000 flat. One form, reviewed within 48 hours. No one is turned away for being unable to prove income.

Will prices go up?

Prices are reviewed every 90 days against real inflation and FX movement, and locked in between reviews. Increases are capped at 5% per cycle, announced 30 days ahead, and existing session bundles are grandfathered at the rate you paid. There are no automatic recurring charges.

Can I pay for a family member's therapy from abroad?

Yes. International cards are accepted with a transparent 3.9% surcharge shown at checkout. The booking belongs to the client receiving care, the therapist communicates only with them, and session content is never shared with the person paying. The sponsor receives a booking confirmation and nothing else.

Do sessions expire if I don't use them?

Individual session credits do not expire. Bundle sessions have a defined usage window — 90 days for most bundles, 180 days for the 8-session Tier 3 bundle — because they represent a committed block of work. Unused bundle sessions simply lapse without penalty. We do not chase renewals, and we do not auto-renew bundles.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card payments via Paystack, bank transfer to a dedicated virtual account, USSD from any feature phone without needing data, and optional direct debit for clients who want it. Local gateway fees are absorbed by Gentle Steps.

What is your refund policy?

Session fees are refundable in full if cancelled 24 or more hours in advance. Under 24 hours, half is retained for the clinician's reserved time and the other half is credited to your account. Unused session credits are refundable on request at any time. Genuine emergencies waive the standard policy — tell us what happened and we will work it out.
