Clinical Pathways

Five Areas of Deep Specialisation

Each pathway has its own clinical protocols, outcome measures and supervision requirements. You are matched to a therapist who works in your pathway — not a generalist who will "give it a go."

Grief & Loss

Tier 2/3

For bereavement, anticipatory grief, pregnancy loss, and the disenfranchised grief that comes with estrangement or ambiguous loss. Our grief pathway uses phased, trauma-informed bereavement models — not generic talk therapy.

Who it's for

Adults navigating death, loss, or the end of a significant relationship

Approaches used

Continuing bonds, narrative therapy, phased bereavement work

1:1 sessions Chat / audio / video Grief journaling support

Childhood Trauma

Tier 3

For adults carrying the long-term effects of early abuse, neglect, household instability or adverse childhood experiences. We work at your pace — you will never be pushed to disclose before you are ready.

Who it's for

Adults with complex or developmental trauma, including relational trauma

Approaches used

Phased trauma work, stabilisation first, somatic-informed, parts work

1:1 sessions Video preferred Safety plan included

Substance Recovery

Tier 2

For people in early recovery, contemplating change, or supporting a family member. Our approach is relapse-informed, anti-shame and realistic — because recovery is rarely linear and shame drives people away from help.

Who it's for

Adults in or considering recovery, and family members affected by a loved one's use

Approaches used

Motivational interviewing, relapse prevention, harm reduction, shame resilience

1:1 sessions Chat / audio / video Family session option

Family & Couples

Tier 4

For couples in distress, families navigating conflict or transition, and multi-generational households balancing care and expectation. Our family pathway is built for multiple devices — everyone can join from wherever they are.

Who it's for

Couples, families, co-parents, adult siblings navigating care decisions

Approaches used

Systemic family therapy, emotion-focused couples work, communication skills

Multi-device sessions 75 min extended format Joint + individual options

Corporate Wellness

Tier 4

For organisations that want to move beyond a poster in the break room. We offer confidential employee therapy, manager training, critical incident debriefs and workplace mental health strategy — priced per organisation, not per buzzword.

Who it's for

Employers, HR teams, NGOs and professional bodies

What's included

Employee sessions, manager briefings, policy support, outcome reporting

How You Meet

Session Formats Built for Real Life

Not everyone has a private room, unlimited data and a working camera. Choose the format that fits — you can change it any time.

Secure Chat

5–10 MB per session

Best for shared rooms and low data. Text-based, asynchronous window, with real-time back-and-forth.

Audio-Only

15–20 MB per session

No camera, no visual pressure. Voice-only sessions that still carry the full relational depth of therapy.

Low-Bandwidth Video

150–250 MB per session

Our recommended default. Reduced resolution to survive unstable networks, without losing the face-to-face connection.

HD Video

500–800 MB per session

Full quality for clients on stable Wi-Fi or fibre. Best for trauma work where non-verbal cues matter most.

Included in Every Pathway

What You Get, Regardless of Service

Verified, Matched Therapist

Matched within 24 hours based on your pathway, language and availability. Free switching if it's not the right fit.

Safety Screening

Risk screening before your first session. If indicators appear, the clinical lead is alerted and a safety plan is made.

Outcome Tracking

Pathway-specific measures reviewed at sessions 1, 3 and 6. If you're not improving, we adjust the plan — not let you drift.

NDPA 2023 Privacy

Health data treated as sensitive personal data. Granular consent. Notes never shared with whoever paid for the session.

Between-Session Resources

Pathway-specific worksheets, grounding exercises and reading — because therapy is more than the hour you spend in session.

Free Rematch Guarantee

If the therapist isn't right, request a rematch from your dashboard — no charge, no penalty, no awkward explanation required.
